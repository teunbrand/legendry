# Guide gizmos

Guide gizmos are a speciality guide components that are very specific to
one or a few aesthetics to display.

Typically they can be
[composed](https://teunbrand.github.io/legendry/reference/guide-composition.md)
with other guides or guide
[primitives](https://teunbrand.github.io/legendry/reference/guide-primitives.md)
to form a complete guide.
