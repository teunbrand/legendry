# legendry: Extended Legends and Axes for 'ggplot2'

A 'ggplot2' extension that focusses on expanding the plotter's arsenal
of guides. Guides in 'ggplot2' include axes and legends. 'legendry'
offers new axes and annotation options, as well as new legends and
colour displays.
